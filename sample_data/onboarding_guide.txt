Onboarding Guide - Acme Pvt Ltd (fictional sample document)

Laptop: Every new employee receives a laptop on the first day from the IT desk on the ground floor.

Buddy program: Each new joiner is paired with a buddy for the first 30 days.

Working hours: Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees may work from home on Wednesdays.

Wi-Fi: Connect to the Acme-Staff network using your company email login.
