Deployment Guide - Acme Pvt Ltd (fictional, Engineering and Admin only)

Every release is deployed to the staging environment first and tested for one day before production.

Production deployments are frozen on Fridays. Emergency fixes need approval from the engineering manager.

To roll back a failed deployment, run the rollback script with the previous release tag and notify the on-call engineer in the incident channel.

The on-call engineer rotates every Monday at 10 AM.
